Step 1: The Prep

Gather all your fresh ingredients on the counter. Ensure your pan is completely dry before adding oil.

  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 3 Cloves Garlic (Smashed)
  • 1 lb Fresh Tomatoes

Step 2: Sauté Aromatics

Turn the stovetop heat to medium. Add garlic to the cold oil to infuse the flavor gently as it heats up.

  • Sauté for 3 minutes until fragrant
  • Do not let the garlic turn dark brown

Step 3: Simmer Sauce

Crush the tomatoes into the pan by hand. Bring to a light boil, then lower heat to simmer uncovered.

  • Simmer for 20 minutes
  • Season with sea salt and fresh basil
